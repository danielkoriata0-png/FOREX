Education System

Five Pillars,
Every Term Defined

Nothing is assumed. Every concept is explained plainly first, then practised on real charts.

🧱

1. Market Basics

What it is: the foundation of forex. A currency pair shows how much of one currency buys another. A pip is the smallest standard price move, a lot is trade size, and spread is the broker's cost.

Why it matters: you cannot manage risk without knowing these units.

📈

2. Technical

What it is: reading raw price action with no indicators and building a narrative, the story of where price came from and where it is likely going.

You will learn: market structure, supply and demand zones, imbalance, liquidity and liquidity periods.

🏦

3. Fundamental

What it is: the reasons behind moves. Central banks (BoE, Fed, BoJ), interest rates, inflation, jobs data and risk sentiment.

Why it matters: news explains the why, price confirms what the market is doing.

🛡️

4. Risk Management

What it is: deciding how much you can lose before you trade. Risk per trade, stop loss, risk-to-reward and drawdown.

Why it matters: it keeps you in the game long enough for your edge to show.

🧠

5. Psychology

What it is: controlling fear, greed and FOMO. Thinking in probabilities, accepting losses and journaling every trade.

Why it matters: most losses come from behaviour, not from analysis.

🧾

Every Topic Is Taught In 5 Steps

1. What it is. 2. Why it matters. 3. How to spot it on a chart. 4. The common mistake. 5. A practice task.
